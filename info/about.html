About Simple Library System (SLS)

Welcome to SLS, your local library service designed to make borrowing books easy and accessible.

Our mission is to provide a comprehensive catalog of books for users to browse, search, and borrow. Whether you're a student, researcher, or casual reader, we have something for everyone.

Features

  • Browse and search through our extensive book collection
  • Borrow books for up to 21 days
  • Track your borrowing history
  • Easy registration and account management
